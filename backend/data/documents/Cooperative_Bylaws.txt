Model Cooperative Society By-laws.

Membership: Any person aged 18 years and above, residing in the operational area of the society, can become a member by paying a share capital of Rs. 1000 and an admission fee of Rs. 100.

Grievance Redressal: Disputes regarding membership must be submitted in writing to the Board of Directors, who shall constitute a 3-member committee to resolve it within 30 days.
